## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-trust, institutional yet deeply human perspective on modern energy storage. Merging modern minimalism with tactile, ultra-clean surface design, the interface feels less like an industrial utility and more like a high-end medical or urban lifestyle technological breakthrough.

The visual tone is defined by clarity, quiet confidence, and spacious breathing room. It prioritizes pristine whitespace, subtle tonal layering, and vibrant emerald accents to convey vitality, efficiency, and environmental harmony. The experience should evoke a sense of calm reliability, forward-looking engineering, and immaculate precision for institutional partners, urban commuters, and critical equipment operators alike.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure light, crystalline depth, and targeted energetic focus:

- **Surface Architecture:**
  - Base canvas: `#ffffff` (Pure, clean white)
  - Surface subtle: `#f8fafc` (Soft porcelain slate for secondary page zones and nested backgrounds)
  - Surface muted: `#f1f5f9` (Subtle boundary wash and input foundations)
  - Surface border: `#e2e8f0` (Whisper-soft division strokes)

- **Energy Accents:**
  - Primary Energy: `#00b870` (Crisp, active mint-emerald indicating optimal state, vital metrics, and primary calls-to-action)
  - Deep Emerald: `#059669` (Grounded viridian used for interactive active states, high-contrast badges, and progress indicators)
  - Energy Tint: `#ecfdf5` (Subtle active indicator washes, tags, and status fills)

- **Text & Neutral Contrast:**
  - Headings & Primary Copy: `#0f172a` (Deep institutional slate offering authoritative clarity without the harshness of pure black)
  - Secondary / Supportive Copy: `#334155` (Balanced slate for descriptions and sub-labels)
  - Muted / Meta Text: `#64748b` (Tertiary captions, timestamps, and secondary units)

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans uniformly across display, text, and data points to provide geometric precision tempered with warm, open counters. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) create punchy, editorial presence. Keep hero headlines concise and paired directly with ample vertical whitespace.
- **Body & Editorial:** Normal tracking and generous line heights ensure seamless readability across clinical white backgrounds.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** For state indicators, metric categories, and micro-badges, use `label-caps` in uppercase format with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to balance the soft geometric nature of the typeface.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column grid system built around sweeping breathing room and editorial composition rather than high-density utility grids.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-width content frame of 1320px, 12 columns with 24px gutters and 40px outer canvas margins. Generous padding around product photography heroes (80px–120px vertical section spacing).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Multi-column metric displays consolidate into 2-column paired cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px margins. Product cards transition into full-width carousels or single-column stacked showcases with comfortable 16px inter-element gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through ultra-diffused atmospheric shadows and delicate structural borders rather than aggressive dark elevation.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#ffffff` or `#f8fafc` with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Modules):** Pure white container placed on `#f8fafc`, framed by a single 1px outline of `#f1f5f9` paired with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Containers):** Raised lifestyle and product showcase cards use an intensified soft bloom: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, slightly elevating 2px on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Controls):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, giving floating telemetry HUDs and mobile navigation drawers soft separation without harsh boundaries.

## Shapes

The design system embraces human-centric geometric curves. Standard interactive elements and inner cards utilize balanced, generous corners:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** 8px (`0.5rem`) for a tailored, crisp feel.
- **Structural Cards & Viewports (`rounded-2xl`):** 16px to 24px (`1rem` to `1.5rem`), lending product photography containers and modular dashboards a modern, approachable curvature.
- **Tags, Pills, and Chips:** Fully rounded circular radiuses (`9999px`) to emphasize lifestyle fluidity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in primary emerald `#00b870`, white typography, 0.5rem radius, height 48px, horizontal padding 24px. Hover state deepens to `#059669` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Background `#f1f5f9`, deep slate text `#0f172a`. Hover state switches background to `#e2e8f0`.
- **Ghost:** Pure transparent background with 1px border `#e2e8f0` and `#0f172a` text. Hover adds `#f8fafc` fill.

### Cards & Photography Displays
- **Product Showcase Card:** Constructed with pure white surface, 16px to 24px (`rounded-2xl`) outer border radius, 1px `#f1f5f9` border, and Level 1 ambient shadow. Ample internal padding (minimum 32px). Product photography is staged on clean, desaturated lifestyle or clinical white backgrounds with floating spec pills.
- **Telemetry / Metric Card:** Features an uppercase label (`label-caps`) in `#64748b`, high-contrast numeral in `#0f172a` (`headline-md`), and inline micro-indicator (e.g., solid emerald dot with pulse effect) indicating real-time system health.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chip:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding 6px 12px, font style `label-md`. Positive/Active uses `#ecfdf5` background with `#059669` text and a 6px solid emerald status dot.
- **Category Filter:** Pill-shaped, light slate border `#e2e8f0`, white surface; selected state utilizes `#0f172a` fill with pure white typography.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#f8fafc`, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`. Focus state transitions to pure white `#ffffff` background with 1.5px `#00b870` border and a diffuse emerald glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 184, 112, 0.12)`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px height/width, 4px border radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Unchecked border is 1.5px `#cbd5e1` over white; checked state fills with `#00b870` featuring crisp white iconography.

### Additional Elements: Metric Gauge Bar
- Seamless, 6px thin track in `#f1f5f9` with an animated `#00b870` fill bar to represent capacity and efficiency ratings without visually cluttering the clean white UI.
